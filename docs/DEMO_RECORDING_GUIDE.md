## Recording Timeline & Script (Total: 2m 45s)

### Part 1: The Problem & Baseline Dashboard (0:00 – 0:35)
- **Visual:** Open `http://127.0.0.1:8000/` with Lumen Payments selected.
- **Presenter Script:**
  > "Hi everyone, I'm Ajay Rathod representing Team Shunya Code. Every growing engineering team faces this problem: we make architectural decisions, but months later, nobody remembers *why* they were made, who decided them, or what breaks if we change them.
  >
  > This is **WhyGraph / RECALL**, an organizational brain built on FalkorDB. 
  > Let's ask: *'Why did we move billing off the old queue, and what breaks if we undo it?'*"
- **Action:** Click the first example chip. Watch the SVG graph light up with the traversed path.
- **Presenter Script:**
  > "Notice how WhyGraph doesn't guess or hallucinate. It traverses the live FalkorDB graph of decisions, evidence, and systems. Every statement carries a clickable source tag, and expanding the receipt shows the exact parameterized Cypher FalkorDB executed in just 12 milliseconds."

### Part 2: Impact Lab & Change Simulation (0:35 – 1:15)
- **Visual:** Click `Impact Lab ↗` in the top navigation header to open `/impact-lab.html`.
- **Presenter Script:**
  > "To help engineering leads evaluate changes before they ship, we built **Impact Lab**. 
  > Here in Scenario 1, we simulate rolling back the Billing Service to the legacy RabbitMQ queue.
  > Let's execute the simulation against FalkorDB."
- **Action:** Click the cyan `⚡ Execute Simulation on FalkorDB` button.
- **Presenter Script:**
  > "FalkorDB traverses multi-hop dependency paths up to 4 hops away. The blast radius table immediately surfaces affected services, hop distances, and native FalkorDB PageRank centrality scores. 
  > It also maps the accountable stakeholders—showing exactly who owns the affected systems and needs to review this change."

### Part 3: Stale Evidence & Invalidation Propagation (1:15 – 1:45)
